Privacy Policy

VFileServer Google Drive - Personal Use

Data Access

This application accesses the owner's Google Drive files and metadata solely to provide bidirectional synchronization with a privately operated NAS.

Data Storage

Synchronized files are stored locally on the owner's NAS. OAuth credentials and synchronization configuration are stored privately on that device.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or disclose it to third parties. Google Drive data is transferred only as necessary for synchronization with Google services.

Data Control

The owner controls the synchronized files and may revoke the application's Google account access through Google Account security settings.
